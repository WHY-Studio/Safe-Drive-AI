User Data Protection


Our software requires access to your system and backend — so it's only natural to ask: How can you trust that your data remains private?

Safe-Drive is provided via our servers, but it does not actively access your backend data. The connection exists solely to enable real-time functionality and processing — not to view or store your content.

Important: Our servers do not see what happens in your backend. The software is only linked through our system, not directly embedded — which means:

  • Your data remains fully under your control
  • Your backend stays 100% private and protected
