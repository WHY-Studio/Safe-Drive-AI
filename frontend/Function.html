How it Works

Analysis
Automatically detect toxic content.

Our AI continuously scans all incoming content and reliably filters out toxic comments and posts to ensure a safe digital environment.

Automation
Responds faster than ever before.

Thanks to automated processes, Safe-Drive can instantly respond to detected toxic content and take appropriate action – more efficiently and faster than manual methods.

Cross-platform
Supports all types of platforms.

Safe-Drive is compatible with the largest platforms and enables centralized management and monitoring of content across various social networks.

Instruction






  1. Subscribe to a Safe-Drive plan
    Visit our website and choose the Safe-Drive subscription that best suits your needs. After subscribing, your personal API key will be generated and available in your dashboard.

  2. Configure your settings
    Go to your user dashboard and adjust your preferences:
    • Choose which content types should be monitored (e.g., comments, messages, posts).
    • Set toxicity threshold levels.
    • Enable automatic alerts if desired.

  3. Integrate the API into your system
    Use our well-documented API endpoint to connect Safe-Drive to your platform. Integration requires just a few lines of code and works with any programming language or infrastructure.

  4. Activate real-time detection
    Once integrated, Safe-Drive immediately starts monitoring all incoming content. Each comment or message is analyzed in real time within milliseconds.

  5. Automatic reporting of toxic content
    If toxic, hateful, or discriminatory content is detected, Safe-Drive instantly reports it – along with relevant user data – to the respective platform provider.

Result: Your platform becomes safer, more efficient, and ethically aligned – with no manual effort required.
